Eczema: An Ayurvedic Perspective on Skin Wellness

Understanding skin health through individualized lifestyle and Ayurvedic wellness guidance.

Blog Skin Disease

Eczema is a group of conditions that can cause dry, itchy and inflamed skin. Management often involves appropriate skin care and medical guidance. Ayurveda may be considered as complementary individualized wellness care, without assuming that every eczema pattern has the same cause or treatment.

What Is Eczema?

Eczema can involve dryness, itching, redness and irritation, with symptoms that may come and go. Different forms of eczema can have different triggers and presentations.

Ayurvedic Individualization

Traditional Ayurveda emphasizes assessment of the person's overall constitution, symptoms, digestion, routine and lifestyle. Any Ayurvedic plan should therefore be individualized rather than based on a single remedy for everyone.

Supporting the Skin Barrier

Gentle cleansing, regular use of an appropriate moisturizer and avoidance of known irritants can be important for people with dry or eczema-prone skin. Fragrances and harsh products may aggravate symptoms in some individuals.

Lifestyle Factors

Sleep, stress, climate, clothing and exposure to irritants can all be relevant to a person's experience of eczema. Keeping a record of flare patterns may help identify practical triggers.

When to Seek Medical Advice

Seek medical evaluation if symptoms are severe, rapidly worsening, associated with significant pain or fever, or if the skin appears infected. Do not delay prescribed treatment while trying complementary approaches.
