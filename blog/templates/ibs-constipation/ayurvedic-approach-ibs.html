BlogIBS & Constipation

Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) is commonly associated with abdominal pain or discomfort, bloating and changes in bowel habits. Some people mainly experience constipation, while others experience loose stools or alternating bowel patterns.

From an Ayurvedic perspective, digestive health is considered in the context of the whole person. An Ayurvedic assessment may consider digestion, appetite, bowel habits, food patterns, sleep, activity, stress and individual constitution.

Understanding IBS

IBS is a functional gastrointestinal disorder, and symptoms can vary significantly between individuals. Food patterns, stress, sleep and changes in routine may influence symptoms in some people.

IBS should be distinguished from conditions that can cause similar digestive symptoms. Persistent or concerning symptoms deserve appropriate medical evaluation.

How Ayurveda Looks at Digestive Health

Ayurveda traditionally looks at digestion as an important part of overall wellbeing. Rather than using one identical approach for everyone, an Ayurvedic practitioner may consider the person's symptoms, daily routine, dietary habits and broader health history.

Lifestyle and Diet

  • Keep meal timings reasonably regular.
  • Prefer fresh, balanced meals that suit your individual tolerance.
  • Stay adequately hydrated according to your needs and medical advice.
  • Include appropriate physical activity.
  • Maintain a consistent sleep routine and work on stress management.

Personalized Ayurvedic Care

Ayurvedic care is individualized. A qualified practitioner can review the pattern of symptoms and overall health before suggesting dietary, lifestyle or traditional Ayurvedic measures.
