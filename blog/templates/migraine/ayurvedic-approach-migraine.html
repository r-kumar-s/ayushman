Ayurvedic Approach to Migraine: An Overview

Understanding migraine and headache patterns through individualized lifestyle and Ayurvedic wellness guidance.

Blog Migraine & Headache

Migraine is a neurological condition that can cause recurrent headaches along with symptoms such as nausea and sensitivity to light or sound. Appropriate medical assessment is important, while an individualized Ayurvedic wellness approach may consider daily routines, digestion, sleep, stress and other contributing factors.

What Is Migraine?

Migraine is more than an ordinary headache. Episodes may involve moderate to severe head pain and can be accompanied by nausea, vomiting, or sensitivity to light and sound. Some people also experience an aura, such as visual or sensory changes, before or during an episode.

How Ayurveda May Look at Migraine

Ayurveda traditionally considers health through an individualized assessment of digestion, daily routine, sleep, stress, diet and other lifestyle factors. Rather than treating every headache pattern in the same way, an Ayurvedic consultation may focus on the person's overall pattern and contributing factors.

Why Individual Assessment Matters

Migraine patterns differ from person to person. Triggers, frequency, duration and associated symptoms can vary considerably. Keeping a headache diary can help identify patterns involving meals, hydration, sleep, stress, screen exposure or other routine factors.

Lifestyle Factors Worth Reviewing

Regular sleep, adequate hydration, consistent meals, appropriate physical activity and practical stress-management habits may support general wellbeing. Changes should be individualized, particularly when migraines are frequent or disruptive.

When to Seek Medical Evaluation

A new, unusually severe or rapidly changing headache should be medically assessed. Urgent evaluation is especially important when headache is associated with symptoms such as weakness, confusion, fainting, fever with neck stiffness, significant vision changes or a sudden severe onset.
