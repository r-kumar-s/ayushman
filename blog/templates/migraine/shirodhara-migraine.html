Shirodhara and Migraine: An Individualized Ayurvedic Overview

Understanding migraine and headache patterns through individualized lifestyle and Ayurvedic wellness guidance.

Blog Migraine & Headache

Shirodhara is an Ayurvedic procedure in which a steady stream of liquid is traditionally directed over the forehead for a period of time. It is used in Ayurveda as part of individualized care, but it should not be presented as a guaranteed treatment or cure for migraine.

What Is Shirodhara?

Shirodhara is a traditional Ayurvedic procedure involving the controlled pouring of a selected liquid over the forehead. The choice of material, duration and suitability may vary according to the practitioner's assessment.

Shirodhara and Migraine Care

Some Ayurvedic practitioners may consider Shirodhara within a broader individualized wellness plan for people experiencing recurrent headaches. The evidence for specific migraine benefits is limited, so expectations should remain realistic.

Why Assessment Comes First

Before any procedure is considered, it is important to understand the person's headache pattern, medical history, current medicines and possible red-flag symptoms. Not every person with a headache is an appropriate candidate for every Ayurvedic procedure.

Ayurveda as Part of a Broader Plan

Ayurvedic care may include discussion of sleep, diet, digestion, stress, daily routine and other lifestyle factors alongside any selected therapy. Any prescribed medical treatment should continue unless the treating clinician advises otherwise.

When Professional Medical Advice Is Important

Seek prompt medical attention for a sudden severe headache, a new neurological symptom, headache after significant injury, fever with neck stiffness, confusion, fainting or a substantial change in an established headache pattern.
