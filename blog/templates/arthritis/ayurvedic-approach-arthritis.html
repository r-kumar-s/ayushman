Ayurvedic Approach to Arthritis: What to Know

Understanding arthritis and joint health through an individualized Ayurvedic perspective.

Blog Arthritis & Joint Health

Arthritis is a broad term used for conditions affecting the joints. Joint pain, stiffness and reduced mobility can have different causes and may vary significantly from one person to another.

What Is Arthritis?

Arthritis refers to inflammation or other disorders affecting one or more joints. Different forms of arthritis can have different causes, symptoms and treatment requirements.

Persistent joint pain or swelling should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional so that the underlying condition can be appropriately assessed.

Ayurvedic Perspective on Joint Health

Ayurveda traditionally takes an individualized approach to health. When discussing joint concerns, an Ayurvedic practitioner may consider factors such as digestion, daily routine, activity, sleep, diet, age and the person's overall health pattern.

There is no single Ayurvedic treatment that is appropriate for everyone with arthritis. The approach should be based on individual assessment.

Common Joint-Related Concerns

  • Joint stiffness
  • Knee discomfort
  • Reduced range of movement
  • Difficulty with everyday activities
  • Joint discomfort after prolonged inactivity

Why Movement Matters

Appropriate movement can help maintain mobility and support overall physical wellbeing. Exercise should be selected according to age, fitness level, joint condition and medical advice.

Diet and Lifestyle

Ayurveda gives attention to regular meals, digestion, sleep, activity and daily routine. A balanced diet and appropriate physical activity can support general health.

When Should You Seek Medical Advice?

Severe pain, significant swelling, fever, sudden loss of movement, injury-related pain or rapidly worsening symptoms require appropriate medical evaluation.

Ayurvedic Consultation

An Ayurvedic consultation can help assess lifestyle and wellness factors and determine whether Ayurvedic care may be appropriate alongside necessary medical management.
