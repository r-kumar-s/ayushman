Heartburn and Indigestion: An Ayurvedic Overview

Understanding acidity and gastritis through an Ayurvedic perspective, with attention to diet, lifestyle, digestion and individualized care.

Blog Acidity & Gastritis

Heartburn may feel like a burning sensation behind the breastbone, while indigestion may involve fullness, bloating, discomfort or nausea after eating.

These symptoms can occur for different reasons, so persistent or frequent symptoms should be appropriately evaluated.

Understanding Heartburn and Indigestion

Heartburn is commonly associated with stomach contents moving back into the oesophagus. Indigestion is a broad term that can describe several upper digestive symptoms.

Symptoms that are frequent, severe or associated with warning signs deserve professional assessment.

Ayurvedic Perspective

Ayurveda traditionally considers digestion and Agni, or digestive capacity, as important aspects of digestive wellbeing. An Ayurvedic practitioner may consider constitution, food habits, routine, stress, sleep and individual symptoms.

An individualized Ayurvedic approach should be based on assessment and should not replace appropriate medical evaluation.

Diet and Lifestyle Considerations

Depending on the individual, helpful foundations may include regular meals, suitable portions, mindful eating, adequate sleep, hydration, appropriate physical activity and attention to known triggers.

  • Regular meal timing
  • Mindful eating
  • Suitable portion sizes
  • Adequate sleep
  • Stress-management practices
  • Appropriate daily activity

When Should You Seek Professional Advice?

Frequent or persistent heartburn should be evaluated appropriately. Severe chest pain, difficulty swallowing, repeated vomiting, vomiting blood, black stools or unexplained weight loss require prompt medical attention.

Do not stop or change prescribed medicines without consulting the treating healthcare professional.
