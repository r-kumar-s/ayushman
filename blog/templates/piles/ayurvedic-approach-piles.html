What Are Piles or Hemorrhoids?

Piles, also called hemorrhoids, are swollen blood vessels and supporting tissues around the lower rectum or anus. They may occur internally or externally and can be associated with bleeding, itching, discomfort or swelling.

Common Symptoms

Symptoms can vary. Some people notice bright-red bleeding during bowel movements, itching, irritation, a lump or discomfort around the anal area. Persistent or recurrent bleeding should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional.

Piles and Ayurveda

Ayurveda approaches digestive and bowel concerns by considering the person's overall health pattern. An Ayurvedic practitioner may review digestion, bowel habits, diet, hydration, activity, sleep and other individual factors before suggesting supportive care.

Persistent, recurrent or unexplained rectal bleeding should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional rather than assumed to be caused by piles.

Why Individualized Care Matters

The appropriate approach can differ according to symptoms, duration, bowel habits and general health. Ayurvedic guidance should complement appropriate medical assessment when needed.

Healthy Bowel Habits

Avoiding prolonged straining, maintaining adequate hydration, eating a balanced diet with suitable fibre, and responding to the natural urge to pass stool may support healthy bowel habits.

Ayurvedic Consultation

An Ayurvedic consultation may help identify lifestyle and digestive factors that could be contributing to recurrent bowel discomfort and develop an individualized wellness plan.

A Holistic Approach

The aim of supportive Ayurvedic care is to encourage healthy digestion, regular bowel habits and overall wellbeing while recognizing when medical evaluation is appropriate.
