PCOD and Weight Management: Ayurvedic Approach

Ayurveda · PCOD / PCOS · Lifestyle

Changes in body weight can occur alongside PCOD or PCOS and may sometimes make hormonal and metabolic concerns more difficult to manage. A sustainable approach generally focuses on overall lifestyle rather than short-term weight-loss methods.

Understanding Weight and PCOD

Weight changes in PCOD can be influenced by several factors, including lifestyle, physical activity, sleep, diet and metabolic health.

Ayurvedic Approach

Ayurveda considers digestion, daily routine and individual constitution when developing a lifestyle and dietary approach. The objective is to create sustainable habits appropriate for the individual.

Helpful Lifestyle Habits

  • Follow regular meal timings.
  • Prefer fresh, balanced meals.
  • Reduce excessive processed foods.
  • Include regular physical activity.
  • Maintain adequate and consistent sleep.
  • Manage stress through suitable relaxation practices.

Avoid Extreme Dieting

Very restrictive diets may not be appropriate for everyone. Sustainable nutrition and lifestyle changes are generally more useful than short-term approaches.

Personalized Ayurvedic Guidance

An Ayurvedic practitioner can assess the individual's symptoms, lifestyle and health history before recommending dietary changes, therapies or other supportive measures.
