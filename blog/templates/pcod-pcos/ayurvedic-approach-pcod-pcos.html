Ayurvedic Approach to PCOD/PCOS: What to Know

Ayurveda · PCOD / PCOS · Women's Health

PCOD and PCOS are commonly associated with irregular menstrual cycles, hormonal changes, acne, weight changes and difficulties with ovulation. Ayurveda approaches these concerns by looking at the individual's overall health, digestion, lifestyle, menstrual pattern and other contributing factors.

Understanding PCOD and PCOS

PCOD and PCOS are often used interchangeably, although they are not exactly the same. The symptoms and underlying factors can vary significantly from one person to another.

Common concerns may include irregular periods, unwanted hair growth, acne, weight changes and challenges related to fertility.

How Ayurveda Looks at PCOD/PCOS

Ayurveda considers menstrual health as part of overall physiological balance. An Ayurvedic assessment may consider digestion, daily routine, sleep, stress, physical activity, diet and individual constitution.

Lifestyle and Diet

Regular daily routines can be an important part of supportive care. Depending on the individual, an Ayurvedic practitioner may recommend improvements in meal timing, physical activity, sleep and stress management.

  • Maintain regular meal timings.
  • Prefer fresh and balanced meals.
  • Include appropriate physical activity.
  • Maintain a consistent sleep routine.
  • Work on stress management and relaxation.

Personalized Ayurvedic Care

Ayurveda emphasizes individualized care rather than applying the same treatment approach to every person. An Ayurvedic consultation can help understand the person's symptoms, lifestyle and health history before suggesting an appropriate plan.
