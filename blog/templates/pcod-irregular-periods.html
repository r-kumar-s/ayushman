PCOD and Irregular Periods

Irregular periods are commonly reported by people with PCOD or PCOS. Menstrual cycles may become longer, shorter or unpredictable, and ovulation may not occur regularly.

However, irregular periods can have many possible causes. Persistent changes in menstrual patterns should therefore be appropriately evaluated.

Ayurvedic Perspective

Ayurveda traditionally considers the individual as a whole. During an Ayurvedic consultation, factors such as menstrual history, digestion, lifestyle, sleep, stress and other individual characteristics may be considered.

Why Menstrual History Matters

Tracking menstrual cycles can help identify patterns in cycle length, bleeding and associated symptoms.

This information can also be useful when discussing menstrual concerns with a qualified healthcare professional.

Lifestyle Considerations

Regular sleep, balanced nutrition, appropriate physical activity and a sustainable daily routine can support general wellbeing.

Lifestyle recommendations should be appropriate for the individual's health status and circumstances.

When Should You Seek Medical Evaluation?

Very heavy bleeding, severe menstrual pain, prolonged absence of periods, unexpected bleeding or other concerning symptoms should be evaluated by an appropriate healthcare professional.
