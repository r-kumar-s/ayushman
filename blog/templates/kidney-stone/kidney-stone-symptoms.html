Common Symptoms of Kidney Stones

Kidney stones may cause sharp pain in the side or back that can move toward the lower abdomen or groin. Some people experience pain that comes and goes in waves. Nausea, vomiting, urinary urgency, burning during urination or blood in the urine may also occur.

Not every urinary symptom is caused by a kidney stone. Urinary infection and other conditions can produce similar symptoms, so diagnosis should not be based on symptoms alone.

Symptoms That Need Prompt Attention

  • Severe or rapidly worsening pain
  • Fever or chills along with urinary symptoms
  • Repeated vomiting or inability to keep fluids down
  • Difficulty or inability to pass urine
  • Significant or persistent blood in the urine
  • Symptoms in someone with a known urinary obstruction or other significant urinary condition

Do not ignore fever with suspected kidney stones. A blocked urinary system combined with infection can be serious and requires prompt medical attention.

Where Ayurveda May Fit

An Ayurvedic consultation may consider the person's digestion, hydration, diet, routine, constitution and medical history. However, it should complement appropriate diagnosis and medical care rather than replace it.

Understanding the Cause

Kidney stone formation can be influenced by factors such as low fluid intake, dietary patterns, family history and certain medical conditions. The type of stone also matters when preventive advice is being considered. Medical evaluation can help identify the appropriate next steps.
