What Are Kidney Stones?

Kidney stones are hard deposits that can form when certain substances in urine become concentrated and crystallize. Their size and location can vary, and some stones may pass without major symptoms while others can cause significant discomfort.

Because urinary symptoms can have several causes, a proper medical assessment is important when symptoms are new, severe or persistent.

An Ayurvedic Perspective

Ayurveda traditionally looks at the individual as a whole rather than treating a symptom in isolation. An Ayurvedic practitioner may consider digestion, hydration habits, diet, daily routine, constitution, medical history and current medicines when discussing an individualized wellness plan.

Hydration and Daily Habits

Maintaining appropriate fluid intake is an important part of general urinary health. The amount of fluid a person needs can vary according to climate, activity, medical conditions and clinical advice. A sustainable routine, adequate sleep and suitable physical activity may also support overall wellbeing.

Important: Ayurvedic care should not be used to delay evaluation of severe urinary pain, fever, vomiting, difficulty passing urine or other concerning symptoms.

When to Seek Medical Evaluation

Seek appropriate medical care for severe or worsening flank or abdominal pain, fever or chills, repeated vomiting, blood in the urine, difficulty passing urine, or symptoms that do not settle. Kidney stones can sometimes cause obstruction or infection and may require urgent treatment.
