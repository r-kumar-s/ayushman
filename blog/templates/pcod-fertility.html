PCOD and Fertility: What Should You Know?

Understanding the relationship between PCOD, ovulation and fertility and the importance of individualized care.

PCOD and Ovulation

PCOD and PCOS may be associated with irregular ovulation in some individuals. Because ovulation plays an important role in conception, irregular cycles can sometimes make conception more difficult.

Fertility is influenced by many factors affecting both partners. PCOD should therefore not be considered the only possible explanation for difficulty conceiving.

Understanding Fertility

Fertility assessment may consider menstrual history, ovulation, reproductive health and other factors.

Appropriate medical evaluation can help identify possible causes when conception does not occur as expected.

Ayurvedic Perspective

Ayurveda traditionally takes an individualized approach to health. An Ayurvedic practitioner may consider overall health, lifestyle, digestion, sleep, stress and other individual factors when discussing supportive care.

Preconception Health

Preparing for pregnancy can involve attention to nutrition, sleep, physical activity, lifestyle habits and appropriate medical care.

People planning pregnancy should discuss relevant health concerns and medicines with their healthcare professionals.

When Should You Seek Fertility Evaluation?

If pregnancy is not occurring despite regular unprotected intercourse, appropriate fertility evaluation may be recommended. Earlier evaluation may be appropriate in some circumstances depending on age, medical history and symptoms.
