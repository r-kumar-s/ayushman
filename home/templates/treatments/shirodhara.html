Shirodhara Treatment in Ahmedabad

Shirodhara is a traditional Ayurvedic therapy involving a gentle continuous flow of selected liquid over the forehead, provided according to an individual's treatment requirements.

Treatments Shirodhara

What is Shirodhara?

Shirodhara is a traditional Ayurvedic therapy in which a selected liquid is gently and continuously poured over the forehead for a period of time.

The word Shirodhara is derived from the Sanskrit words Shiro, meaning head, and Dhara, meaning continuous flow.

Why Do People Choose Shirodhara?

People may consider Shirodhara as part of an Ayurvedic wellness or treatment program for relaxation, stress, mental fatigue, sleep-related concerns or general wellbeing.

Individual experiences and outcomes vary.

How is Shirodhara Performed?

The practitioner selects the appropriate preparation and treatment approach based on the individual's needs.

During the session, the selected liquid is gently and continuously poured over the forehead in a controlled manner.
