Treatments Arthritis

Understanding Arthritis

Arthritis is a broad term covering different conditions affecting joints. Common types include osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, gout and psoriatic arthritis.

Different forms of arthritis can have different causes and require different medical approaches. Proper diagnosis is therefore important.

Common Arthritis Symptoms

People with arthritis may experience joint pain, stiffness, swelling, reduced movement, difficulty walking, morning stiffness or discomfort during activity.

Ayurvedic Approach to Joint Health

Ayurvedic care may include individualized diet guidance, lifestyle recommendations, Abhyanga, Swedana, selected local therapies, Panchakarma where appropriate and other traditional Ayurvedic therapies.

The exact approach depends on the individual's condition, symptoms and Ayurvedic assessment.

Ayurvedic Care for Arthritis and Joint Concerns

Ayurvedic treatment is traditionally individualized according to the person's symptoms, constitution, lifestyle and overall health.

Depending on the assessment, therapies such as Abhyanga, Swedana or other Ayurvedic procedures may be considered. Dietary and lifestyle guidance may also form part of the treatment plan.

When Should You Seek Medical Evaluation?

Severe or sudden joint swelling, fever, unexplained weight loss, significant injury, inability to bear weight or rapidly worsening symptoms require appropriate medical assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions About Ayurvedic Arthritis Care

Can Ayurveda help with arthritis?

Ayurvedic treatment may provide supportive care for some people with arthritis, depending on the underlying condition.

Is Ayurvedic treatment the same for all arthritis?

No. Arthritis has different forms and treatment should be individualized.

Can I continue my existing arthritis medicine?

Do not stop prescribed medication without discussing it with your treating doctor.

Can Abhyanga be used for joint concerns?

Abhyanga may be considered as part of an individualized Ayurvedic plan when appropriate.

Can Panchakarma be used for arthritis?

Selected Panchakarma procedures may be considered for some individuals after appropriate Ayurvedic assessment.
