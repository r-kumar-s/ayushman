Personalized Panchakarma Care in Ahmedabad

Panchakarma is one of the important therapeutic approaches described in classical Ayurveda. It involves a planned sequence of therapies selected according to an individual's health status, Ayurvedic constitution and treatment needs.

At Ayushmaan Bhavah in Ahmedabad, Panchakarma is not treated as a one-size-fits-all procedure. The treatment plan is developed after understanding the person's health concerns, lifestyle, diet, previous medical history and suitability for specific therapies.

Depending on the individual assessment, Panchakarma may be incorporated with other traditional Ayurvedic therapies and lifestyle recommendations.

What is Panchakarma?

Panchakarma is a classical Ayurvedic therapeutic approach that uses selected procedures to support balance and wellbeing. The therapies used depend on the individual's condition and Ayurvedic assessment.

Abhyanga

A traditional Ayurvedic oil therapy involving the application and massage of selected oils according to individual needs.

Learn About Abhyanga

Swedana

A traditional Ayurvedic sudation therapy that may be used as part of an individualized treatment plan.

Shirodhara

A traditional Ayurvedic therapy involving a controlled, continuous flow of selected liquid over the forehead.

Learn About Shirodhara

Basti

A specialized Ayurvedic procedure that may be recommended for selected individuals when considered appropriate by the Ayurvedic practitioner.

Nasya

A traditional Ayurvedic therapy involving the administration of selected preparations through the nasal route when clinically appropriate.

Individualized Therapies

Additional Ayurvedic therapies may be incorporated according to the individual's health requirements and treatment plan.

Who May Consider Panchakarma?

Panchakarma may be considered as part of an individualized Ayurvedic care plan for people seeking support for various health and wellness concerns.

  • Digestive and gastrointestinal health concerns
  • Musculoskeletal discomfort
  • Stress and mental fatigue
  • Lifestyle-related health concerns
  • General wellness and rejuvenation programs
  • Selected chronic health concerns where Ayurvedic care is considered appropriate

Panchakarma is not automatically suitable for every person. A consultation and individual assessment should be completed before beginning treatment.
