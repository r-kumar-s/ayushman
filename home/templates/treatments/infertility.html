Personalized Ayurvedic consultation for women and men experiencing fertility and reproductive health concerns, with an individualized approach to overall wellbeing.

Treatments Fertility

Understanding Fertility and Infertility

Difficulty conceiving can have many possible causes affecting either partner. Factors may include ovulation problems, age-related fertility changes, reproductive conditions, endometriosis, sperm-related factors, hormonal conditions, lifestyle factors or other medical conditions.

Sometimes no single cause is immediately identified. Appropriate fertility evaluation is important when conception is taking longer than expected.

Ayurvedic Approach to Fertility

Ayurvedic consultation may consider diet, digestion, sleep, stress, lifestyle, menstrual health, reproductive history, general health and existing medical treatment.

Based on the assessment, personalized dietary, lifestyle and Ayurvedic recommendations may be provided as part of a broader fertility and wellness approach.

Fertility Care for Women and Men

Women may seek consultation for irregular menstrual cycles, PCOD or PCOS-related concerns, ovulation-related concerns or preconception wellbeing.

Male fertility can also be affected by sperm count, sperm motility, lifestyle, nutrition, stress and certain medical conditions. Both partners should be considered when evaluating fertility.

Fertility and Preconception Health

Preconception health involves looking at the overall health of both partners before pregnancy. Nutrition, sleep, physical activity, stress management and management of existing health conditions can all be relevant.

An individualized Ayurvedic consultation can discuss lifestyle and wellbeing as part of a broader preconception approach.

When Should You Seek Fertility Evaluation?

If pregnancy has not occurred after an appropriate period of trying, professional fertility evaluation is recommended. Earlier evaluation may be appropriate depending on age, medical history or known reproductive concerns.

Ayurvedic consultation can be considered alongside appropriate gynecological, urological or fertility-specialist care.

Ayurveda Alongside Fertility Treatment

People undergoing fertility treatment may wish to discuss complementary Ayurvedic care with their fertility specialist and Ayurvedic practitioner.

Inform all healthcare providers about medicines, supplements and therapies you are using.

Frequently Asked Questions About Ayurvedic Fertility Care

Can Ayurveda help with infertility?

Ayurvedic care may provide supportive individualized care for some fertility-related concerns, but infertility can have many causes and should be medically evaluated.

Should both partners be evaluated?

Yes. Fertility difficulties can involve either partner, so assessment of both partners is often appropriate.

Can I combine Ayurveda with fertility treatment?

Discuss all Ayurvedic medicines and supplements with your fertility specialist and Ayurvedic physician before combining treatments.

Does PCOD cause infertility?

PCOD or PCOS can affect ovulation in some women, but it does not mean that pregnancy is impossible.

Can men receive Ayurvedic fertility consultation?

Yes. Fertility concerns can affect either partner, and men may also seek individualized consultation for reproductive health and fertility-related concerns.

What reports should I bring?

Previous blood tests, ultrasound reports, semen analysis, prescriptions and other relevant fertility reports can be useful during consultation.
