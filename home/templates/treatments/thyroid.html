Understanding Thyroid Disorders

The thyroid is a gland involved in regulating several important functions of the body, including metabolism and energy use. Thyroid disorders can affect people in different ways.

Common thyroid-related conditions include hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, thyroiditis, thyroid nodules and goitre. Proper medical evaluation and appropriate testing are important when thyroid disease is suspected.

Common Thyroid-Related Symptoms

Depending on the underlying condition, people may experience fatigue, changes in body weight, appetite changes, feeling unusually cold or hot, hair changes, dry skin, sleep problems, mood changes or changes in bowel habits.

These symptoms can have many causes and should not be used alone to diagnose a thyroid disorder.

Can Ayurveda Replace Thyroid Medicine?

Ayurvedic consultation should not be considered a reason to stop prescribed thyroid medication.

If you are taking thyroid medicine, continue it as prescribed unless your treating physician advises otherwise. Ayurvedic care can be discussed as a complementary approach with appropriate medical supervision.
